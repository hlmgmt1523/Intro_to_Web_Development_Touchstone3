Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2012 when two neighbors began selling sourdough from a folding table at the Saturday farmers market. Word spread, lines grew, and a year later we opened our storefront on Main Street. More than a decade later, we still bake everything by hand each morning, and we still know most of our customers by name.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts with great ingredients. Our flour is stone-milled from Minnesota-grown wheat and rye, our butter and eggs come from family farms within 100 miles, and our seasonal fruit comes from local orchards. Buying local means fresher ingredients for you and a stronger community for all of us.

Meet Our Staff

Maria Smith - Head Baker and Co-Founder

Mariah has been shaping our sourdough since the farmers market days. She's in the kitchen by 3 a.m. every morning and still hand-feeds the same starter she began with in 2012.

Mike Johnson - Pastry Chef

Mike brings ten years of pastry experience to every croissant and danish we sell. His seasonal fruit creations change with whatever local orchards have that week.

Emily Davis - Cake Designer and Events Coordinator

Emily works one-on-one with families and event planners to design custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and community celebrations. If you have an idea, she'll help bring it to life.
